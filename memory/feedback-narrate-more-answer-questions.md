**Why:** During fast-moving operations (a port bouncing between rejected/canceled/locked/resubmitted states), terse status lines left him in the dark. He is not a telecom/dev expert — he needs the story, not just the state. And direct questions getting indirect or missing answers is a trust-breaker.

**How to apply (all projects):**
1. When something has multiple moving parts or changes state rapidly, narrate: what just happened, why, what happens next, what (if anything) he needs to do — in plain English, every time.
2. Scan every message for "?" — each question gets its own explicit answer, first, before anything else.
3. When a process stalls or goes weird, explain the weirdness rather than just reporting the status code.
